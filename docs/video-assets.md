# Khutwa — submission video assets

For the 2–3 minute SMAC submission video. Three parts:
A) image-generation prompts, B) image-to-video prompts, C) the voice-over script.
All AI-drafted — review before use (see `docs/AI-USAGE-REPORT.md`).

---

## A. Image-generation prompts

### Master style (prepend to every prompt)

> Cinematic still, warm golden-hour light just before dusk in the UAE. Colour
> palette: bone-sand `#F7F3EC`, deep Ghaf-green `#3E6B4F`, warm amber-gold
> `#DC9E43`, soft muted sky. Fine film grain, gentle haze, shallow depth of
> field, calm and editorial, unhurried. No text, no logos, no UI unless asked.
> 16:9, high detail.

### Shot list (generate each; 2–3 variations)

1. **Hero — the lone Ghaf tree.**
   A single wide, umbrella-canopy Ghaf tree on a low sand dune at dusk, long
   soft shadow across the sand. Far in the frame, small, a family of three
   (grandmother, parent, child) walking toward it. Vast calm sky. Sense of
   belonging and scale.

2. **Generations walking together.**
   A grandfather and a young grandchild, seen from behind, walking hand in hand
   along an old coral-stone heritage wall in Abu Dhabi, warm low light catching
   the stone. Intimate, quiet, unposed.

3. **The app in the hand.**
   Close, over-the-shoulder: a hand holding a modern phone showing a calm app
   home screen — a small illustrated green tree above a thin circular progress
   ring — the screen glowing softly warm. Background: a family park at golden
   hour, softly blurred.

4. **The tree blooming.**
   The same Ghaf tree, now haloed in warm gold light, tiny luminous motes /
   seed-pods drifting upward around the canopy, the sand faintly glowing. A
   feeling of a moment shared. Magical but restrained, not fantasy.

5. **Every step waters the story (metaphor).**
   A trail of bare footprints pressed into warm sand, and where the footprints
   lead, the prints visually become fine roots that feed a small green sapling
   pushing out of the dune. Symbolic, clean, minimal.

6. **Together.**
   Two phones held near each other on a low majlis cushion at dusk, both
   screens showing the same small tree, both softly blooming with warm light.
   Hands of two different people. Shallow focus.

7. **Map of memory (optional B-roll).**
   A soft aerial of Abu Dhabi's coastline at dusk with 6–8 warm points of light
   marking heritage places (Qasr Al Hosn, the Corniche, the Grand Mosque, Al
   Maqta), faint dotted walking paths linking them.

## B. Image-to-video prompts

Feed each generated still into an image-to-video model. Keep motion **subtle** —
4–6 second clips, slow, no fast cuts.

1. **Hero:** very slow push-in toward the tree; the canopy sways gently in a
   light breeze; a few grains of sand drift across the foreground; the distant
   family takes two or three steps forward. Locked, cinematic, no shake.

2. **Generations walking:** slow dolly-follow behind the pair as they walk two
   steps; light flickers on the stone wall; grandchild looks up briefly.

3. **App in the hand:** gentle parallax — background park drifts, the phone
   stays steady; the tree on screen does one soft sway; the progress ring fills
   a little; a faint warm glow pulses once.

4. **Blooming:** the motes rise and fade upward in a slow loop; the gold halo
   breathes once (expand, settle); the canopy sways once. Dreamlike, slow.

5. **Footprints to roots:** slow track along the footprints; the transition
   from print to root grows/creeps forward as the camera moves; the sapling
   nods.

6. **Together:** almost still; both screen-trees sway in sync; the warm bloom
   light swells gently on both at the same moment; a thumb shifts slightly.

7. **Map:** slow drift over the coastline; the points of light kindle one by
   one; the dotted paths draw themselves between them.

## C. Voice-over script (~2 min 30 s, calm, warm)

Aim for an unhurried pace, ~140 wpm. Pauses marked `( … )`. Pair each block
with the shot number in brackets.

---

**[Shot 5 → 1]  (0:00–0:20)**
Every family leaves a trail. ( … ) In the UAE, most of us aren't walking it
enough — and the time we do spend together is getting shorter, while the
stories our grandparents carry go unrecorded. ( … ) Movement and memory are
fading at the same time.

**[Shot 1]  (0:20–0:38)**
Khutwa treats a walk as the reason to be together — not the goal. ( … ) One
family. ( … ) One shared Ghaf tree — the tree that survives the desert by
sending its roots deep. ( … ) Every step your family takes waters it.

**[Shot 3]  (0:38–1:05)**
Your combined steps, pulled from the phone's health data, grow the tree through
visible stages. ( … ) Walk near a place that matters to your family — a fort, a
mosque, a stretch of the Corniche — and the app quietly unlocks a short story
about that spot, drawn from UAE heritage, and reads it aloud.

**[Shot 4 → 6]  (1:05–1:30)**
And when your family is actually *together* — in the same place, at the same
time — the tree blooms. ( … ) That bloom is the whole point: a small, shared
moment you can see.

**[Shot 3, screens]  (1:30–1:58)**
The home screen shows one number — the Khutwa Score — built from three
strands: Root, for how much you move; Bloom, for the memories you unlock
together; and Heritage, for the places you preserve. ( … ) There's a walk view,
a log of every story you've unlocked, and a family screen — where location
stays on the device, nothing leaves the family group, and the whole app works
in English or Arabic.

**[Shot 2]  (1:58–2:18)**
The AI has one narrow job: turn a place and a family's own memory into a warm,
short spoken story. ( … ) Every story is written ahead of time, reviewed by us,
and cached on the phone — no live tracking, no data sent away. Responsible by
design.

**[Shot 1, pull back]  (2:18–2:30)**
This is the UAE's Year of the Family. ( … ) Khutwa turns an ordinary walk into
something a family keeps. ( … ) Khutwa. ( … ) Every step waters the story.
